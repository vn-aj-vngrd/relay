# Story template direction — 17 September 2026

The job is a shareable game image, edited on a phone after or beside play.
The user's reference images suggest connected detail photos, star-accented
scrapbooks, and compact-camera photo windows. Their source screenshots are visual
references, not product requirements or assets to reproduce.

Spotify describes its [2025 Wrapped design](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-12-03/wrapped-marketing-campaign/)
as personal imagery with a reduced palette and a mix of analog and digital
materials. [Strava's activity-sharing guide](https://support.strava.com/en-us/articles/15401840-sharing-your-strava-activities)
places relevant activity statistics alongside a selected photo or map.

Relay interpretation: give the recorded result one strong typographic focal
point, retain readable context, and offer photo arrangements with distinctive
materials. No copied branding, fabricated routes, or invented performance data.
Inter handles headings; the existing mono stack handles precise stats; Caveat
remains the diary caption face. Existing game colors, paper, ink, and cream frames
remain the palette rather than adding another color system.

The subsequent simplification removes the explicit suggestion panel. Look owns
theme and color; Layout starts with three arrangements and reveals two additional
ones through More layouts. Recorded photo-memory points and matches move into a
compact opaque overlay, replacing the duplicate row below the photo. The caption,
personal results, and game context stay outside the image. Empty games have no
overlay. Both SVG and PNG share the same geometry and numeric values.

All themes remain available. All new Stories start in Court Pop; users can select another theme in Look. Unit and browser regression cases are authored. Validation deferred
to pre-commit; no rendered-device or export fidelity claim is made yet.


Story reassessment: a single framed custom photo uses the recorded-stats band;
collages keep totals in the factual text area so no image is covered. Full-photo
backgrounds use a continuous text composition without an allocated artwork gap
or decorative theme artwork. Empty photo memories omit zero-match summaries;
unrecorded court time is explicit, and standings retain the existing result order.
The recap label is Game recap at every time of day. Validation deferred to pre-commit.


The final public names are Court Pop, Studio, Scrapbook, Soft Serve and Clubhouse.
Stable IDs are retained. Soft Serve replaces the large bow/scallop composition
with serif captions, quieter numerals and one ribbon. Clubhouse replaces multiple
colored stripes with precise ticket framing and sans-serif sporting type. Studio
retains a graphic-free performance layout; Scrapbook retains its simplified mat;
Court Pop remains the bold default. The landing uses five labeled examples from
the actual renderer, and removes the obsolete mock controls. All sample facts are
explicitly disclosed as sample game data. Help Center copy uses the same names.
