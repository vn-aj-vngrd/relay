# Metro Manila and Davao booking-source verification

Checked September 16, 2026. This is remote documentary verification of public venue booking data, not a claim that every pin was visited or independently surveyed.

## Import-ready sources

### PickleHub: 136 verified additions, 4 held candidates

[PickleHub](https://picklehub.ph/clubs) is an operating booking platform. Its public homepage loads the anonymous `get_clubs_listing_data` RPC. The public application maps the RPC's `latitude`, `longitude`, `club_address`, `total_courts`, `custom_url_slug`, `visibility_status`, and `account_type` directly into its court listing/map. The source is a venue booking record, rather than a third-party editorial directory.

Public extraction endpoint observed in the site's application bundle:

`https://odimuhhyzyzdymujymzy.supabase.co/rest/v1/rpc/get_clubs_listing_data`

The endpoint is a read-only listing request invoked by the public app with its public publishable key. No login, private account tables, booking records, personal profiles, or privileged credentials were used. Raw public venue response is retained for this session at `/tmp/picklehub-clubs.json`; observed application code at `/tmp/picklehub.js`. Do not commit the whole application bundle or personal/business account metadata from unrelated platforms.

Source snapshot contained 220 listings: 142 active and 78 coming soon. All were full booking accounts. Only active accounts with positive court inventory and supplied coordinates were considered. Coming-soon venues are excluded rather than represented as currently operating.

The 142 active source records cover Davao City (69), Tagum City (21), General Santos (15), Midsayap (6), and other Mindanao cities, plus one Minglanilla and one Lapu-Lapu listing. The source inventory has:

- No repeated exact coordinate pairs or repeated exact venue names.
- No missing coordinate pairs or coordinates outside Philippine bounds.
- No detected mismatches exceeding 100m between numeric coordinates and explicit Google place coordinates where the source supplied a full Google Maps URL.
- No coordinates generated by this research, geocoded municipality centers, copied photos, reviews, or editorial descriptions.

The snapshot is `data/courts/metro-booking-2026-09-16.json`. All source coordinates are retained at their published precision; decimal precision alone is not treated as verification. Verification rests on the active operator booking record and its venue address/map fields. Each verified record stores its own public booking URL, location-source URL, checked date, and the method's limitation.

Example bookable venue independently found by web search: [MatchPoint Pickleball](https://picklehub.ph/matchpoint-pickleball?tab=home), Domsat Road, Davao, with ten courts and a live court-by-court booking schedule. Different source renderers can return the SPA shell rather than rendered content; the public listing response is the reproducible fact source.
