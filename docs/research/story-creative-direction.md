# Research: Relay Story creative direction

> Implementation boundary: Relay retains Minimal unchanged and adds the four expressive treatments below with the existing selectable Pink palette. This research informs original artwork; it is not an engagement study or a claim of official organic-Story safe zones. The static implementation and synthetic-browser evidence are recorded in `docs/audits/GAME_MOBILE_RESPONSIVENESS.md`. GIF/video remains research only.

## Summary
Build a small editorial system, not five backgrounds: one dominant factual metric, strong type hierarchy, a recognizable pickleball composition, and optional real photography. Retain **Minimal (default), Scrapbook, Coquette, Court Pop, Retro Rally**, with **Pink as a palette option**, not a sixth layout. The direction below draws on inspected first-party imagery; it is design recommendation, not measured evidence of engagement or current trend popularity.

## Findings

1. **Wrapped separates expressive framing from readable information.** In Spotify’s actual [Top Songs image](https://storage.googleapis.com/pr-newsroom-wp/1/2024/12/Top5Songs-1-1024x576.jpg), blue/purple stepped graphics surround a yellow field; the final list uses black ranking numerals, aligned square album images, and consistent text rows. The [summary-share image](https://storage.googleapis.com/pr-newsroom-wp/1/2024/12/SummaryShareCard-2-1024x576.jpg) repeats the same photo, two-column lists, listening total, and footer across different color treatments. **Transfer:** give Relay a stable information grammar and reserve expressive shapes for framing. Do not imitate Spotify’s proprietary typeface, hyperloops, artwork, or lockup. Spotify itself describes playful layouts and vivid color as art direction, not an experimentally proven recipe. [Official design account](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2024-12-04/10-years-spotify-wrapped/)

2. **Editorial rhythm is more useful than ornamental volume.** Spotify’s inspected sequence moves from a short centered statement to a graphic interlude to a structured factual list. The summary then compresses the story into a recognizable composition. **Transfer:** within a static Relay card, establish three beats—small identity/context, very large result, quiet supporting facts—rather than filling every gap. A single metric as the visual hero is our recommendation; Spotify’s summary itself contains several metrics and is not evidence that every shareable needs exactly one. [Top Songs image](https://storage.googleapis.com/pr-newsroom-wp/1/2024/12/Top5Songs-1-1024x576.jpg)

3. **Strava makes the visual accent carry factual meaning.** Its inspected Year in Sport graphic, “The Z Is for Zippy,” uses a black field, oversized white headline, orange highlights on key findings, white comparison bars, an orange highlighted cohort, and small shoe/bicycle icons in colored category labels. The orange is repeated where the claim is supported, rather than scattered for decoration. This is a report/share infographic, not a personal activity Story; it is too dense to transplant intact. **Transfer:** highlight the actual featured value and use one small pickleball-specific marker; any chart must correspond to real data. [Official report page, January 2024, covering 2023](https://stories.strava.com/articles/strava-year-in-sport-trend-report-insights-on-the-world-of-exercise) · [Inspected graphic](https://stories.strava.com/cms/assets/https%3A%2F%2Fimages.secure.ctfassets.net%2F7ajcefednbt4%2F4LD3QW36qApVMYNoiIkAC2%2F7722c100dd51bb0bf4b15ef9dd9c588c%2FStrava-Share-Asset-12-GenZ-Running-Behavior.jpg)

4. **Sport identity can celebrate participation without inventing performance.** Strava’s official product update describes selecting a real weekly streak, choosing among four sticker designs, and sharing it. Its 2024 report also explicitly frames activity around connection and balance, not solely maximal performance. **Transfer:** celebrate recorded sessions, games, or time played when those are available; do not manufacture wins, skill ratings, streaks, “MVP,” or rankings. Four designs around one factual streak support the idea of bounded expression, but no inspected evidence establishes which design performs best. [Product update](https://stories.strava.com/articles/whats-new-on-strava-new-languages-annual-best-efforts-and-weekly-streak-stickers) · [2024 report](https://press.strava.com/en-gb/articles/strava-releases-annual-year-in-sport-trend)

5. **Instagram’s examples use personal imagery as the creative material.** The inspected Frames example places a slightly tilted, white instant-print frame over a full-bleed scene, with a caption and timestamp in its border. Cutouts places a recognizable person over a friends’ photo with short angled text. These compositions feel authored because the people and memory are specific—not because there are many abstract embellishments. **Transfer:** optional user-owned court/group photos; one clear crop; a caption rail outside the image; a small original paddle/ball illustration when photography is absent. Do not reproduce Instagram’s UI or imply that a flat exported image has interactive stickers. No cutout-generation feature is required. [Official announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/05/new-stickers-in-instagram-stories/) · [Frames image](https://about.fb.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/02_Frames.jpg) · [Cutouts image](https://about.fb.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/04_Cutouts.jpg)

6. **UI clearance must be designed, not left to chance.** Instagram’s inspected examples visibly reserve space for the account header and bottom messaging controls. Meta’s official safe-zone guidance concerns ads, where overlays differ from organic shares; it should not be quoted as a universal organic Story measurement. **Relay recommendation:** start at 1080×1920 with critical content inside x=72–1008 and y=288–1536 (roughly 7% sides, 15% top, 20% bottom). Let only nonessential court lines/color bleed outside. This is a proposed working inset, not an official guarantee; inspect actual organic Story placement before finalizing. [Official safe-zone guidance, search-accessible](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/980593475366490)

## Recommended bounded original five-theme system

**Shared contract:** same factual payload and reading order across all five. Small session/player identity → dominant recorded value with explicit unit → at most two secondary facts → real session title/date/venue when available → quiet Relay signature. Keep exact names; wrap rather than replace them with invented slogans or illegible tiny text. Theme names belong in the picker, not in place of factual session names. Missing data stays absent; decoration never impersonates a chart, score, or achievement.

| Existing theme | Original composition and type | Motif/photo treatment | Pink treatment and restraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Minimal — default** | Editorial court scorecard: oversized upright sans numeral aligned to an asymmetric court-line grid; small unit, clear lower metadata rail. Whitespace creates hierarchy, not an empty text-on-color poster. | One cropped court corner with a meaningful non-chart ball mark; optional straight-edged photo strip separated from text. | Blush field, near-black type, deep raspberry accent. No gradients or extra sticker scatter. |
| **Scrapbook** | Court-day keepsake: one dominant paper stat slip, one offset photo window, orderly date/venue caption. Upright factual numerals; handwriting only for a short non-factual accent, if used. | At most two tape tabs and one original paddle/ball stamp. Tilt the photo/backing slightly, not the core stat text. Without a photo, use a small original court sketch—not an empty placeholder. | Rose paper/tape accents and dark ink; texture confined to paper edges, never over glyphs. |
| **Coquette** | Pickleball club invitation: large elegant display numeral, simple sans labels, a centered court-ticket panel with generous interior space. Do not use script for data or names. | One original line-drawn ribbon tied around a paddle handle, plus a fine scalloped frame. Optional oval photo below the metric. The sport object anchors the style. | Pink/cream with burgundy text. No piles of bows, hearts, pearls, or tiny ornamental labels. |
| **Court Pop** | Graphic match poster: giant condensed upright number, asymmetric hard-edged color blocks, strong small label, compact factual footer. | One oversized cropped perforated-ball silhouette and court-line geometry; optional hard rectangular photo window. No fake trajectory visualization. | Hot pink with dark plum and one light neutral; no rainbow gradient, confetti field, or floating blob collection. |
| **Retro Rally** | Vintage recreation-club score ticket: chunky display numeral, compact sans/mono metadata, horizontal baseline stripes and a restrained printed border. | One original paddle emblem, not an invented award seal. Optional rectangular photo with a clean cream border. | Dusty pink/cream/oxblood alternative to warm cream/green/orange. Keep wear/grain out of the factual panel. |

**System limits (recommendations, not benchmark results):** one primary illustration, one photo maximum, two decorative accents maximum, and at most two font families per theme. Give the main value roughly three to five times the secondary text size, while retaining sufficient room for long factual names. Theme distinctions must survive grayscale: grid scorecard / layered keepsake / framed invitation / asymmetric poster / striped ticket—not merely palette swaps.

**Anti-slop acceptance criteria:**
- First glance identifies the statistic and unit; second glance identifies whose session and when.
- All five read as pickleball without requiring the Relay wordmark.
- No generic motivational filler, unearned “champion” badge, fake charts, or decorative numbers.
- The Pink option remains readable and recognizably different in each theme.
- A photo never obscures facts or crops through the important subject; no-photo mode looks intentionally finished.
- At phone preview size, names and secondary facts remain legible; longer names do not force microscopic text.
- Remove any motif that contributes neither sport identity nor hierarchy. Do not confuse minimalism with blankness or maximalism with creativity.

## Gaps
Actual imagery was inspected for Spotify, Instagram, and a Strava Year in Sport share infographic. Strava personal activity-overlay and weekly-streak sticker compositions were **not** visually inspected; their current layouts should not be inferred from the report graphic. Direct Strava asset access initially failed, but the official site’s CMS proxy supplied the inspected image. No evidence here ranks these themes by popularity, conversion, or engagement; “fun/trendy” remains a creative hypothesis to review with representative Relay users.

No proprietary logo, artwork, template, or licensed image should be incorporated into Relay; images were fetched solely for visual research, not saved into project assets. Original motifs and authorized user photos are the recommended materials. No motion/video implementation is proposed; the existing motion/export research remains separate. Only this managed research artifact was written; no repository source changes or validation commands were run.
