# Relay logo system research

Researched: August 16, 2026

## What enduring product brands do

### Lead with one recognizable primitive

Apple’s app-icon guidance says a successful icon should be unique, memorable, understandable at a glance, and simple. A single clear idea survives reduction better than an illustration or collection of symbols.

Source: [Apple Human Interface Guidelines — App icons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons)

### Prefer the wordmark when space allows

Linear’s official brand guidance says its wordmark has stronger recognition and should be used where space allows. Its symbol is for constrained layouts. Linear also prefers monochrome use and requires generous clear space rather than adding decoration around the mark.

Source: [Linear Brand Guidelines](https://linear.app/brand)

### Make the symbol irreducible

Vercel’s symbol is a single geometric primitive and is reserved for places too tight for the full logo. Its brand system defines clear space from the symbol itself. The lesson is not to copy the triangle; it is to find the smallest shape that remains unmistakably yours.

Source: [Vercel Geist — Brands](https://vercel.com/geist/brands)

### Distinctive silhouettes outlast detail

Nike’s Swoosh succeeds as a freestanding silhouette rather than a literal sports illustration. Nike distributes the mark as a transparent asset, reinforcing that the logo itself—not a decorative tile—is the identity.

Source: [Nike, Inc. Logos](https://about.nike.com/en/newsroom/releases/nike-inc-logos)

## Relay decision

Relay will use a **perforated pickleball** as its single primitive:

- one circular silhouette
- six asymmetric negative-space holes
- optic yellow-green (`#c7db00`) across the product lockup and favicon
- one theme-independent mark
- transparent background and no outline
- paired with the Relay wordmark whenever room permits

This is specific to pickleball without becoming clip art. It avoids the generic letter, miniature court, paddle/search-icon ambiguity, app-icon tile, motion stripes, and borrowed Linear/Vercel geometry explored previously.

## Usage rules

1. Use the mark plus wordmark in headers and sidebars.
2. Use the mark alone only for the favicon, app icon, or genuinely constrained space.
3. Use optic yellow-green for the product mark and favicon. Tournament standards require a uniform ball color but publish no official digital hex; `#c7db00` is Relay’s calibrated screen color for the common optic tournament ball.
4. Preserve clear space equal to one hole diameter around the mark.
5. Do not place the mark inside a circle, rounded square, gradient, or colored badge.
6. Validate every revision at 16, 24, and 64 pixels on light and dark surfaces.
