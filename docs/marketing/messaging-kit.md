# Relay messaging kit

## Core copy

### Short description

One shared link for a pickleball night with friends—plan, RSVP, courts, scores, repayment, chat, and recap.

### Store-style description

Relay keeps a casual pickleball session clear from the first invite to the last photo. Create the game, send one link, let friends RSVP without an account, check in at the court, rotate fair matchups, update scores together, collect each player’s share, and save the night as a recap.

Relay is built for friend games. It does not book courts, move money, run leagues, or create a competitive rating.

### Three proof points

- **One link works first.** Friends see where, when, who is playing, what it costs, and whether a spot remains before signing in.
- **Every court stays clear.** Check in, choose a familiar play style, use an optional round timer, follow the paddle stack, and score in realtime.
- **The night stays together.** Repayment, conversation, photos, results, and a shareable recap remain attached to the session.

## Venue partner pitch

### Subject

Help booked groups organize the rest of game night

### Message

Relay is a free friend-session companion for groups that already book courts with venues like yours. It does not replace your reservation system. After booking, a host creates one shared game link for RSVP, player capacity, court assignments, scores, and collecting each friend’s share.

A small “Organize your group after booking” link or QR can reduce roster and payment questions without changing your operations. We would like to pilot it with a few casual groups and share what we learn.

## FAQ copy

**Does Relay book the court?**

No. Book with the venue as usual, then mark the court confirmed in Relay.

**Does everyone need an account?**

No. A friend can open the shared link, enter their name, RSVP, check in, follow play, chat, and handle their payment as a guest. An account keeps history across games.

**Does Relay collect money?**

No. The host shares GCash, Maya, bank, cash, or custom instructions. Relay records proof and confirmation but never moves money.

**Is this a rating app?**

No. Playing experience only helps make friendlier teams when the host chooses Balanced Mix. Session standings describe that night only.

**Can we use fixed partners?**

Yes. Paddle Stack can keep pairs together, and Team Round Robin lets each fixed pair play every other pair once.

**What happens when the game is full?**

New going responses join an ordered waitlist. If a spot opens, Relay promotes the next eligible player and creates a notification.

## Voice rules

- Say **game**, **session**, **crew**, **court**, **share**, and **recap**.
- Say **repayment** or **your share**, not billing, receivables, or settlement ledger.
- Say **playing experience**, not rating, rank, or skill score.
- Describe a concrete outcome before a feature name.
- Never claim automatic reminders, payment transfer, bookings, or competitive ratings beyond what production actually delivers.
- Use real product screens and real test data. Do not invent testimonials, player counts, venue partners, or usage statistics.
