# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Friends organizing recreational pickleball sessions. Hosts coordinate plans, rosters, costs, courts, and scores; players and lightweight guests act from shared links, often one-handed while messaging, traveling, paying, or standing courtside.

## Product Purpose

Relay is the shared home for a pickleball session: create a game, send one link, keep the plan clear, coordinate payment, rotate players through courts, record the night, and make it easy to play again. Success means a new invitee understands where, when, who, capacity, cost, and booking status before signing in.

## Brand Personality

Clear, spirited, and grounded. The voice is conversational and direct. The experience should feel like a capable friend running the night: energetic when play is live, quiet when the plan is settled, and never overly serious about recreational statistics.

## Anti-references

Not an admin dashboard, sports broadcast, public social feed, court-booking marketplace, tournament manager, or generic SaaS template. Avoid glassmorphism, decorative gradients, oversized radii, excessive pills and cards, abstract sports decoration, meaningless metrics, over-animation, and pasted shadcn defaults.

## Design Principles

1. **The session is home.** Every workflow returns to a single understandable session object.
2. **The link carries the plan.** Public pages provide complete context before authentication and make RSVP lightweight.
3. **Reveal responsibility progressively.** Common actions stay immediate; host controls and advanced configuration appear only when relevant.
4. **Courtside beats comprehensive.** Live Mode prioritizes glanceability, touch targets, deterministic rotations, and resilient shared state.
5. **The session becomes the memory.** Scores, people, photos, and reactions accumulate around the event rather than a generic feed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with semantic structure, keyboard operation, visible focus, 44px minimum touch targets, screen-reader-friendly score controls, non-color status cues, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and clear offline/reconnecting feedback. Public links and guest RSVP must remain usable without an account.
